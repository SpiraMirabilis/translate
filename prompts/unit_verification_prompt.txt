Most annotations are correct — this is a verification pass that only needs to catch the clear errors.

The highlighted word is the Chinese unit that was annotated. The metric annotation itself has been stripped from the sentence so it doesn't bias your judgement.

- The "unit" word is part of a place name: "Li Village", "Zhang Family Manor", "Mu County"

Keep (do NOT flag) when:
- The highlighted word is a real distance/weight/area measurement, even if used hyperbolically ("a thousand li", "ten thousand zhang high")
- Figurative-but-traditional measurement phrases ("thousand-jin burden", "three-chi sword"); these are legitimate idioms grounded in real units
- You are uncertain

Return a JSON array of the integer IDs of the matches that are clearly NOT units. If all matches look legitimate (or you're unsure about all of them), return [].
